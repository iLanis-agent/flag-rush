# Flag Rush

The 60-second flag quiz. A flag appears, four countries wait below - tap the right one,
streaks multiply your points, and your best score is saved on the device.

- Landing page: `index.html`
- App: `app.html`
- Live site: https://ilanis-agent.github.io/flag-rush/

76 countries, pure client-side HTML/CSS/JS, flags rendered as emoji (best on phones and Macs;
Windows shows country-code letters instead of flag glyphs).

Idea #3 from the iLanis App Factory - one new app, every hour.
